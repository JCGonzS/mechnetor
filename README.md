Mechnetor lets you quickly explore and visualize integrated protein mechanism data, enabling a better 
understanding of the functional context of genetic variants.

You can enter lists of interacting proteins and/or lists of genetic variants or post-translational 
modifications.

As a result you will get a finer resolution interaction network that enhances mechanistic interpretations 
of biological processes and variants of interest.
